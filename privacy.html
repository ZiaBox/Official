1. Introduction

GearTrack is an offline studio equipment inventory, asset valuation, and production kit management application developed by ZIABOX LLC. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

GearTrack is designed to operate entirely on your device. The app does not require account registration and does not collect, transmit, or share personal information with the developer or third parties.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

GearTrack does not collect, transmit, or share personal or sensitive information. This includes:

  • Names, email addresses, or phone numbers
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • IP addresses or network information
  • Contacts
  • Photos, camera data, or microphone recordings
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Financial or payment information

The app does not require account registration or user identification.

3. Local Data Storage

GearTrack allows you to create equipment records, asset information, valuation details, maintenance records, and production packing checklists. This information is stored locally on your device.

  • Data remains on your device
  • Data is not accessible to the developer
  • Data is not shared with third parties
  • The app does not send this information to external servers

Local data may include information such as equipment names, serial numbers, purchase prices, depreciation settings, maintenance records, and packing checklists that you choose to enter into the app.

4. Permissions

GearTrack is designed to work offline and does not require sensitive Android permissions. The app does not require:

  • Internet access
  • Location access
  • Camera access
  • Microphone access
  • Contacts access
  • External storage access

All features are designed to work locally on your device.

5. Third-Party Services

GearTrack does not use advertising networks, analytics services, tracking services, or cloud services to collect or transmit user information.

The app operates independently without third-party data collection or external processing of the information you enter.

6. Children's Privacy

GearTrack does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. Because the app does not collect personal information, no personal information is knowingly collected from children under the age of 13.

7. Data Security

Information entered into GearTrack remains stored locally on your device. The app does not transmit this information to external servers. Data security also depends on the security of your device and its operating system.

8. User Control and Data Deletion

You have control over the information stored locally in the app. Depending on the available app features, you may edit or delete individual records and packing information.

  • You can delete information within the app where supported
  • You can clear the app's data through Android settings
  • Uninstalling the app removes its locally stored application data

9. International Data Transfers

GearTrack does not transfer user information to external servers or international locations. Information entered into the app remains on your device.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's functionality or privacy practices change. Updates will be reflected by revising the “Last Updated” date above.
